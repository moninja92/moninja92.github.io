About

Control Systems, Systems Design and Game Design

I'm Maurice Birchard, a current systems engineer moving into the UI and UX side of game design. For years I've designed the screens operators use to run solar plants and industrial systems in real time. That work taught me how to put the right information in front of someone at the exact moment they need it, and how much a cluttered screen can cost in production.

Skills and Capabilities

  • Interface design: data processing, ease of use, and functional design.
  • Design that ships: I prototype in Figma, then build in Unity, Godot or TypeScript, so designs account for how the engine actually works.
  • Team process: wireframes first allows the team to identify early risks and prototype quickly.

Tools

Figma, Unity (uGUI, TextMeshPro), Godot, TypeScript and Vite, Ignition Perspective and Vision, Wonderware System Platform, Miro/Notion.
