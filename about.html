About the designer

I'm Maurice Birchard, a systems engineer moving into the UI and UX side of game design. I've spent years on the screens operators use to run solar plants and industrial systems in real time. That work taught me how to put the right information in front of someone at the exact moment they need it, and how much a cluttered screen can cost.

What I do

  • Interface design: dense information made easy to read and act on.
  • Design that ships: I prototype in Figma, then build in Unity, Godot or TypeScript, so designs account for how the engine actually works.
  • Team process: wireframes first, so the team spots risks early and prototypes quickly.

Tools

Figma, Unity (uGUI, TextMeshPro), Godot, TypeScript and Vite, Ignition Perspective and Vision, Wonderware System Platform, Miro and Notion.
