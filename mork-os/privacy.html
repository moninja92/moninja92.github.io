MORK OS privacy policy

Last updated November 3, 2026

mork-os is a personal application used only by its developer, on the developer's own computer, with the developer's own Google account. This policy explains how it handles Google user data.

What data it accesses

  • Gmail (read-only): sender, subject, date and Gmail's short preview snippet of recent inbox messages. Full message bodies and attachments are not retrieved.
  • Google Calendar (read-only): titles, times and locations of upcoming events on the primary calendar.

How it uses the data

The data is used only to produce a private inbox summary and a list of upcoming events for the account owner. It is processed by an AI model running locally on the owner's computer.

Storage and sharing

  • Summaries are stored only as files on the owner's computer.
  • Google user data is not sold, not shared with third parties, not used for advertising, and not used to train AI models.
  • OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer and are sent only to Google.
  • mork-os has no servers and no other users.

Limited Use

mork-os's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Locally stored tokens and summaries are deleted by removing them from the owner's computer.

Contact

Questions can go to the developer through the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen for this app.
