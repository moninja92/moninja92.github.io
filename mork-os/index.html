MORK OS

A personal project by Moninja

mork-os is a personal, self-hosted assistant that runs entirely on its developer's own Windows PC. It organizes tasks and notes in a local Obsidian vault and coordinates local and cloud AI tools.

Google access

mork-os can connect to the developer's own Google account with read-only access to Gmail and Google Calendar. It uses that access to sort the inbox and list upcoming events, locally, for one person. It cannot send, delete or change email or calendar events.

mork-os is not a public service. There are no accounts and no sign-ups, and it is not offered to anyone else.
